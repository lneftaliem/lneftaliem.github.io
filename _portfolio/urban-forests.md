Developed a deep learning framework to map individual tree crowns from high-resolution satellite imagery across four North American cities. The work examines how tree abundance, canopy cover, and crown size vary across neighborhoods, revealing dimensions of urban forest inequity that conventional inventories and aggregate canopy measures can miss.
