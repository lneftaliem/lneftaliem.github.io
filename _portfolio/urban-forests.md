Urban trees are critical for climate adaptation and human health, but cities often lack detailed information about where trees are, how large they are, and who has access to their benefits. In collaboration with NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, my work uses deep learning and high-resolution satellite imagery to map individual tree crowns across four North American cities, providing a more complete picture of urban trees and revealing dimensions of inequity that conventional inventories and canopy measures can miss.
