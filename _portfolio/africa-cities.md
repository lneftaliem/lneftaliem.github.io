Rapid urban growth is creating major questions about how African cities finance housing, build transportation systems, and govern growth. I helped launch Stanford's Building Africa's Cities Summit to bring researchers and practitioners working on these questions together, securing $37,000 across seven partner organizations to support the inaugural convening.
