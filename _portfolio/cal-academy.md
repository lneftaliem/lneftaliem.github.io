Scientific research has greater public value when people have meaningful ways to engage with it. Working with researchers and educators at the California Academy of Sciences, I developed four live, interactive programs rooted in active Academy research and designed for multigenerational audiences. Each translates current ecological research into a 15-minute facilitated experience and was developed within the Academy's Transformative Education framework.
